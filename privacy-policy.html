FerroLedger — Privacy Policy

Owner: Deepak Dhunna  ·  Effective: October 27, 2026  ·  Version: 1.0

FerroLedger is a single-user personal finance application operated by its owner for his own accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data we collect

Through Plaid, with the owner's consent given in Plaid Link: account metadata (institution, account name, masked account numbers), balances, credit liability details (APR, due dates, minimum payments), transactions, and recurring charges for accounts the owner chooses to connect. Bank credentials are entered only into Plaid Link and are never seen or stored by FerroLedger.

How data is used

Only to operate the dashboard for the owner: displaying balances and due dates, tracking subscriptions and spending, categorizing transactions, generating reports, and powering the owner's AI assistant — only under scoped, expiring grants the owner issues, and only at his direction.

Sharing

Data is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing. Data leaves the application only to Plaid (to retrieve it) and to services the owner explicitly connects.

Retention and deletion

Data is retained while the owner uses the application. The owner may export or permanently delete all data at any time; disconnecting an institution deletes its data and removes the Plaid connection.

Security

See the FerroLedger Information Security Policy: encryption in transit and at rest, passkey + TOTP MFA, private-network-only access, audit logging.

Contact

Deepak Dhunna (owner). This policy is displayed inside the application and is reviewed at every release phase and at least annually.
